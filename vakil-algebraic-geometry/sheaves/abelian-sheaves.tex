\subsection{Abelian categories of sheaves}

Recall that for a sheaf of rings $\O_X$, an {\emphcolor $\O_X$-module} is a sheaf of Abelian groups $\F$ such that each
$\F(U)$ is an $\O_X(U)$-module in a compatible way (\refmath[definition]{sheafmod}).
A sheaf of Abelian groups is a $\ul\bZ$-module, so $\O_X$-modules generalize the concept of sheaves of Abelian groups much like
how modules generalize the concept of Abelian groups.

The goal of this section is to demonstrate that the category of $\O_X$-modules (and in particular sheaves of Abelian groups)
forms an Abelian category.

To do this we need to show that the category of $\O_X$-modules has kernels and cokernels, and that monics are kernels and
epics are cokernels.
Firstly let us consider $\Mod^\pre_{\O_X}$, the category of presheaf $\O_X$-modules.
The definition here is the same as \refmath[definition]{sheafmod} except we require only that $\F$ be a presheaf.

Now $\Mod^\pre_{\O_X}$ is complete and cocomplete.
If $\Phi\colon I\to\Mod^\pre_{\O_X}$ is a diagram, then there is an obvious forgetful functor
$\Mod^\pre_{\O_X}\to\Ab^\pre_X$.
Composing this with $\Phi$ we can take the co/limit, and then there is an obvious $\O_X$-module structure on the resulting
object (this is the same situation as with $\Mod_R\to\Ab$).

Then sheafification lifts to $\Mod^\pre_{\O_X}\to\Mod_{\O_X}$.
This is clear by considering $\F^\sh(U)$ explicitly.
Thus $\Mod_{\O_X}$ is a reflective subcategory of $\Mod^\pre_{\O_X}$, and since the latter is co/complete so too is the former.

In particular the kernel of a $\Mod_{\O_X}$-morphism is the kernel of it viewed as a morphism in $\Ab^\pre_X$,
and its cokernel is the sheafification of this morphism.

Recall that the stalk of a morphism in $\Mod_{\O_X}$ is an $\O_{X,p}$-module.
Thus we view $\bul_p$ as a functor
$$ \bul_p\colon \Mod_{\O_X} \to \Mod_{\O_{X,p}} . $$
When $\O_X=\ul\bZ$ then $\Mod_{\O_X}$ is the same as $\Ab_X$, and $\O_{X,p}$ is the same as $\bZ$ so that
$\Mod_{\O_{X,p}}\cong\Ab$.
Thus all of the following results hold for sheaves of Abelian groups.

\blemm[name={stalkco/ker}]

    Taking stalks preserves kernels and cokernels: for $p\in X$ and a morphism $\phi\colon\F\to\G$ of $\O_X$-modules,
    $$ (\ker\phi)_p \cong \ker(\phi_p),\qquad (\coker\phi)_p \cong \coker(\phi_p) . $$

\elemm

\bproof

    This is clear for cokernels, as the stalk at $p$ amounts to taking a certain colimit, and colimits (such as cokernels)
    commute with colimits.

    For kernels we can show this directly.
    We view both $(\ker\phi)_p$ and $\ker(\phi_p)$ canonically as subobjects of $\F_p$, in which case it is clear that
    there is an equality.
    \qed

\eproof

\blemm[name={monepionstalks}]

    In $\Mod_{\O_X}$, a morphism $\phi\colon\F\to\G$ is monic iff its stalks are monic, epic iff its stalks are epic,
    and an isomorphism iff its stalks are isomorphisms.

\elemm

\bnote

    It doesn't matter if we view $\phi_p$ as existing in $\Mod_{\O_{X,p}}$ or $\Set$; a module morphism (in the ordinary
    sense) is a monomorphism/epimorphism/isomorphism iff it is injective/surjective/bijective.

    Note that in lieu of this, a $\Mod_{\O_X}$-morphism with any of the above properties has those properties in
    $\Ab_X$ and $\Set_X$.

\enote

\bproof

    First we note that stalks determine a morphism: if $\phi_p=\psi_p$ for all $p\in X$ then $\phi=\psi$.
    This is because the forgetful functor $U\colon\Mod_{\O_X}\to\Set_X$ is faithful, and commutes with taking stalks.
    So $U\phi_p=U\psi_p$, which implies $U\phi=U\psi$ by \refmath[proposition]{stalksdeterminemorphism}, and thus $\phi=\psi$.

    Then the proofs of \refmath[proposition]{isoonstalks} and \refmath[proposition]{epionstalks} are valid here.
    \qed

\eproof

This allows us to prove the desired result.

\bthrm

    $\Mod_{\O_X}$ is an Abelian category. 

\ethrm

\bproof

    As already shown, $\Mod_{\O_X}$ is co/complete.
    Furthermore it is clearly a pre-additive category.
    We need only show that for a monic $\phi$ and an epic $\psi$,
    $$ \phi = \ker\coker\phi,\qquad \psi = \coker\ker\psi . $$
    But this is true only if for all $p\in X$,
    $$ \phi_p = \ker\coker\phi_p,\qquad \psi_p = \coker\ker\psi_p \eqcnt $$
    (parentheses on the left-hand sides of the equations here are unnecessary, as per \refmath[lemma]{stalkco/ker}).
    By \refmath[lemma]{monepionstalks} $\phi_p,\psi_p$ are monic and epic respectively, so \gotoleqn{} holds as
    $\Mod_{\O_{X,p}}$ is Abelian.
    \qed

\eproof

\blemm

    Let $\phi$ be a $\Mod_{\O_X}$-morphism, then $\im\phi$ is the sheafification of the image presheaf of $\phi$.
    In particular $(\im\phi)_p=\im(\phi_p)$.

\elemm

\bproof

    We know that $\im_\pre\phi=\ker_\pre\coker_\pre\phi$, while $\im\phi=\ker\coker\phi$.
    Since $\coker=\coker_\pre^\sh$ and $\ker=\ker_\pre^\sh=\ker_\pre$ so
    $$ \im\phi = \ker_\pre^\sh\coker_\pre^\sh\phi = (\ker_\pre\coker_\pre\phi)^\sh = (\im_\pre\phi)^\sh $$
    as desired.

    The second point follows from stalks commuting with kernels and cokernels.
    \qed

\eproof

\bcoro

    The following sequence of $\Mod_{\O_X}$-morphisms
    $$ \F\xtos\alpha\G\xtos\beta\H $$
    is exact iff
    $$ \F_p\xtos{\alpha_p}\G_p\xtos{\beta_p}\H_p $$
    is exact for all $p\in X$.

\ecoro

\bproof

    The former is exact iff $\im\alpha=\ker\beta$ iff $(\im\alpha)_p=(\ker\beta)_p$ for all $p\in X$, which happens iff
    $\im\alpha_p=\ker\beta_p$, i.e.\ if the latter sequence is exact.
    \qed

\eproof

An immediate consequence of this corollary is the following.

\bcoro

    Taking stalks is an exact functor $\bul_p \colon \Mod_{\O_X} \to \Mod_{\O_{X,p}}$.

\ecoro
