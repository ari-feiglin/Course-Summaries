\subsection{Morse homology}

In this section we discuss Morse homology in more depth.

Our first step is to show that Morse homology is independent of choice of Morse function and Smale gradient.
We can view Morse-Smale pairs on $M$ as a category whose morphisms $f_0\to f_1$ are maps
$F\colon M\times[0,1]\to M$ such that $F_t=f_0$ for $t\leq1/3$ and $F_t=f_1$ for $t\geq2/3$.
This is a groupoid.
It can be shown that Morse homology forms a functor from this to the category of chain complexes
(up to quasi-isomorphism).

Because the codomain is a groupoid, all the Morse complexes are then quasi-isomorphic as desired.
